About the sampling, geometry, and validation

Valid input surfaces are partitioned into planar quads by placing shared edge midpoints and triangle centers. This preserves the piecewise-linear surface. Defective sources undergo winding-number solid reconstruction at a fine voxel resolution before quad conversion. This produces dense, faithful surface meshes; it does not create artist-directed animation edge flow. Disconnected solid components are retained.

Validation checks all-quad faces, two incident faces per edge, coherent winding, vertex manifoldness, positive volume, and triangle self-intersections before the exact quad partition. Surface deviation uses 12,000 area-uniform samples in each direction and additional source-vertex samples; these are measurements, not a certified maximum-distance bound. Repair can remove interior overlapping surfaces and very thin features. Inspect each comparison and its report for details.

Each Blender scene contains the final model, a hidden original reference, satin material, three area lights, and three cameras. Geometry is normalized for the studio; original center and scale are stored in the model properties and report. All geometry is baked into the mesh.
