MODEL AND DATA LICENSES

This collection has no blanket license covering the source models. Each model
and its technical mesh conversion retain the original license recorded for that
model in data/sample.json and listed at docs/attribution.html. Creator names,
original Thingiverse design links, source download URLs, and SHA-256 hashes are
included in the manifest. Respect the corresponding NonCommercial, ShareAlike,
and NoDerivatives conditions. License version numbers are not provided by the
dataset catalog; the original design page is the authoritative source.

Technical processing is documented per model in results/<id>/report.json:
normalization, orientation correction, quad partition, and solid reconstruction
or local repairs where necessary. Scenes also include studio materials, lights,
and cameras. No new permission to adapt the source designs is granted here.
